# AI Product Teardowns  
*Short analyses of AI products through a product, UX, and positioning lens*

## Why this repository

I’m documenting how I analyze AI products — especially conversational systems — focusing on how they are designed, positioned, and experienced by users.

My goal is to better understand what makes AI products truly usable and scalable beyond the underlying models.

---

## Example: AI Voice Agents

### What works well

- Clear and focused use cases (e.g. support, assistant, sales)
- Fast response time and low perceived latency  
- Structured interaction flows that guide the user  
- Consistent tone and personality  

### Common gaps

- Weak personality design leading to generic interactions  
- Poor error handling and recovery flows  
- Unclear boundaries of what the system can or cannot do  
- Over-reliance on model capability instead of product design  

---

## Key takeaway

The quality of an AI product is not only defined by the model, but by how well it is translated into a coherent and usable experience.
